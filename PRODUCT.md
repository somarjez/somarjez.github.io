# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and potential collaborators evaluating Jezreel Ramos
in a 10–30 second first scan, usually from a LinkedIn or GitHub link, often on
mobile. They need to quickly understand what he does, see evidence of real work,
and find a way to reach him. Secondary audience: fellow developers and community
peers.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that positions Jezreel as a **Data Analyst · Web Developer ·
Project Management · AI** practitioner and proves it with live, verifiable
evidence (real GitHub repos and metrics, named projects, credentialed
certifications). Success = a visitor leaves able to say what he does, believes the
evidence, and knows how to contact him.

## Brand Personality

Analytical, precise, builder. Confident without shouting. The voice is exact and
evidence-led: specific nouns and verbs, concrete numbers, no buzzwords. The
terminal/developer identity is a deliberate, committed brand lane (chosen, not
defaulted) — engineer-native, but the data-analyst framing leads. Show, don't
tell: real GitHub data on the page beats adjectives about skills.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI/Bootstrap portfolio templates: stock hero + three identical feature
  cards + pastel gradient + Inter everywhere.
- Corporate/agency stiffness: faceless, buzzword-driven, no point of view.
- Effect-soup: motion and decoration that fight readability.

## Design Principles

- **Show the data, don't claim it.** Live GitHub metrics, real repos, verifiable
  credentials. Evidence is the design.
- **Identity-preservation.** The terminal lane is committed; refine its craft,
  don't restart it. Variants elevate what ships.
- **Earn every element.** No gradient text, no per-section eyebrows, no
  decorative glass — each is an AI tell unless it carries meaning.
- **Scan-first hierarchy.** A recruiter gets the four roles and the proof within
  one fold; depth rewards the curious (⌘K, projects, certs).
- **Precise over loud.** Motion is crisp and purposeful; copy is concrete.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for all text. Every animation (terminal boot, reveals, tilt,
spotlight, counters, data-viz) has a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` fallback.
Command palette is fully keyboard-operable and screen-reader labelled. Pointer-only
effects are disabled on touch / no-hover devices.
